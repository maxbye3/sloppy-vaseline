THE RULES

Sound artificial.
Think human.

  1. Gather at least three players in a room.
  2. One player writes a scenario. Everyone, including the scenario writer, has 60 seconds to write a convincing bit of AI slop.
  3. Vote through groups of up to three human responses and a fresh AI response. Its identity is hidden from everyone until the reveal. Confirm your vote within 30 seconds per group. Each group ends as soon as everyone confirms their vote. Results appear after all groups.
  4. Earn 10 points for each AI response you spot. Earn up to 15 deception points for fooling people, adjusted for exposure.

You can see your own response, but cannot vote for it. Everyone sees every submitted human response once per prompt. Groups are balanced and randomized, with at most four options at a time. If AI generation is unavailable, the round pauses until the host retries. Your answers are saved.
