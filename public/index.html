THE RULES

Sound artificial.
Think human.

  1. Gather at least four players in a room.
  2. One player writes a scenario. Everyone else has 60 seconds to write a convincing bit of AI slop.
  3. Find the AI response on your private ballot. Confirm your vote within 20 seconds.
  4. Earn 10 points for spotting AI. Earn up to 15 deception points for fooling people, adjusted for exposure.
