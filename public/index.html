THE RULES

Sound artificial.
Think human.

  1. Gather at least three players in a room.
  2. One player writes a scenario. Everyone, including the scenario writer, has 60 seconds to write a convincing bit of AI slop.
  3. Find the one AI response among everyone’s submissions. Confirm your vote within 60 seconds. Voting ends early when everyone confirms. Authors stay hidden until the reveal.
  4. Earn 10 points for spotting the AI response. Earn up to 15 deception points for fooling people, adjusted for exposure.

Everyone sees every submitted response plus one AI response on a single ballot. You can see your own response, but cannot vote for it. If AI generation is unavailable, the round pauses until the host retries. Your answers are saved.
